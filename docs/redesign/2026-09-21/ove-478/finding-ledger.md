## How to read this

- **Paths.** Receipts are in `docs/redesign/2026-09-21/` (cited as `OVE-4xx-PROOF.md` plus section and line). Browser specs are `apps/web/tests/<file>:<line> "title"`, with line numbers taken on `main`. Every spec cited is in the gate list `apps/web/scripts/browser-gate-specs.ts` on `main`. Unit tests are under `apps/web/src/`. "Linear <issue> comment <id>" is the release receipt posted on that issue.
- **Closure, per receipts.** *Met*: every clause of the audit's **Acceptance** has direct evidence in a receipt, a gate spec or a recorded check. *Partly met*: at least one acceptance clause has no evidence, or a receipt says it is unmet. Where an acceptance names a method that was never run (user testing, "verify with a screen reader"), the finding is *Partly met*. Where it describes something the DOM decides (reading order, `aria-pressed`, `lang`) and a test asserts the DOM, it is *Met* and the missing listening session is listed as a residual. *Not met*: the primary acceptance is not delivered.
- **OVE-478 column.** *Yes* means the owner's receipt defers something to OVE-478 by number; the quotes are in `deferred-to-ove-478.md`. *Implicit* means the receipt says the session was not performed, or names "final integration", without the number. A dash means the receipt says nothing on it.
- **[source check]** marks a residual found in this review by reading `main`. No receipt states it.
