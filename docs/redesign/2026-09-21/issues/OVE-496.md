## Acceptance criteria

1. Provide prominent common/scientific-name search with explicit plant/animal scope and simple useful filters. Keep browse and technical register distinct.
2. Explain species/form/owned object through labels and context, not a mandatory tutorial. Numeric cultivar names and missing common names remain usable without becoming the default story of the product.
3. Results prioritize honest identity and available real experience; do not fabricate popularity or claim every taxonomy row has journal evidence.
4. Open a reference card for understanding; Add to my garden uses progressive creation, and Write where already owned offers matching specimens first.
5. Preserve typeahead architecture and server/source contract; no second search service, heavy taxonomy download or fake private gate.
6. Empty, loading, search failure, ambiguous names, diacritics and pagination have distinct states and share filter patterns.
